01 — The field
01 / MANY
One field · many approaches

AI is not one thing.
It's a whole field.

Artificial intelligence brings together ideas from computer science, statistics, neuroscience and more. It is the broad effort to build systems that can perceive, learn, generate, reason or act toward a goal.

◎

Perception

Finding useful signals in images, sound, video and the world around us.

⌘

Language

Working with words: understanding, translating, summarising and communicating.

◇

Decision-making

Comparing possible actions and selecting one to move toward an objective.

02 — Machine learning
DATA → MODEL
Learning patterns from examples

Show the system
how the world behaves.

Machine learning is a major part of modern AI. Instead of writing every rule by hand, developers train models to find patterns in examples and use those patterns to make predictions on new information.

↗

Supervised learning

Learn from examples paired with known answers, such as labelled images.

⠿

Unsupervised learning

Find structure in data when explicit answer labels are not provided.

⟳

Reinforcement learning

Improve a strategy through actions, feedback and rewards.

03 — Neural networks
LAYERS
Simple units · connected together

Small signals.
Complex behaviour.

Neural networks are models built from layers of connected computational units. During training, the strengths of those connections are adjusted so the network becomes better at mapping inputs to useful outputs.

04 — Generative AI
CREATE
New outputs from learned patterns

From recognising patterns
to creating with them.

Generative systems produce new content based on patterns learned during training and the context supplied at runtime. They can help people explore ideas, make drafts, build prototypes and work across different media.

✳

Text & code

Draft explanations, transform writing and assist with software development.

▧

Images & video

Generate or transform visual material from descriptions and references.

♫

Audio & speech

Work with speech, sound, music and voice interfaces.

05 — Multimodal systems
SENSE
More than words alone

Different inputs.
Connected context.

Multimodal AI works across more than one kind of information. A system might combine text, images, audio, video or structured data to build a richer representation of a task.

TextImagesAudioVideoSpatial dataSensors
06 — Human perspective
THINK
Capability · context · responsibility

Powerful tools
need thoughtful use.

AI systems can be useful, but they can also be wrong, biased, brittle or difficult to explain. Good use means checking important outputs, protecting private information, understanding limitations and keeping people accountable for decisions.

⌕

Verify

Check important claims against reliable sources and real-world evidence.

◈

Question

Consider bias, uncertainty, privacy and who may be affected.

◎

Stay involved

Use AI to support human judgement, not replace responsibility.
